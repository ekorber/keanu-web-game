Minecraft Browser Edition

Build, explore, and survive in an infinite voxel world

Experience the ultimate sandbox adventure right in your browser. No downloads, no installations - just pure creative freedom and endless exploration.

Upcoming Features

Infinite Worlds

Explore procedurally generated terrain with diverse biomes, caves, and structures. Every world is unique!

Creative Building

Build anything you can imagine with 100+ block types. From simple houses to complex redstone contraptions.

Survival Mode

Gather resources, craft tools, fight monsters, and survive in a challenging environment with health and hunger systems.

Mining & Crafting

Mine valuable ores, craft powerful tools and weapons, and progress from wood to diamond and beyond.

Mobs & Combat

Encounter friendly animals and dangerous monsters. Fight zombies, skeletons, creepers, and epic bosses.

Multiplayer

Join friends in cooperative building or compete in custom minigames. Create communities and share your creations.

Redstone Engineering

Build complex machines, automated farms, and logic circuits with the powerful redstone system.

Multiple Dimensions

Travel to the dangerous Nether and mysterious End dimension. Face the Ender Dragon in an epic boss battle!

Game Modes

Survival

Gather resources, manage health and hunger, and build to survive against hostile mobs.

Creative

Unlimited resources, flight enabled, and instant building for pure creative expression.

Hardcore

Permadeath challenge for experienced players. One life, maximum difficulty.

Technical Excellence

Built with cutting-edge web technology:

  • WebGL 2.0 rendering for smooth 3D graphics
  • Three.js for advanced visualization
  • Optimized chunk loading for seamless exploration
  • 60 FPS target performance
  • Works on desktop and mobile browsers
